# ClientNest validation (Weeks 1–3)

**23 Aug 2026:** interviews are **skipped** so development can proceed. The founder (freelancer) is the first buyer voice. Keep this skill. Do not invent interview rows, and do not recreate `marketing/` or checkout files. The sales notes were removed on 12 Oct 2026.

Likes, generic praise, and developer curiosity are not purchase intent.

Do not add `marketing/` files back. Record any future interview only if the founder asks, and do not invent results.

## Interview script (ask workflow before pitching)

1. How do you currently share project status, files, and approvals with clients?
2. What part of that workflow causes the most repeated work or confusion?
3. Have you ever built a client portal? How long did it take?
4. Which features would make a starter kit usable for a real client project?
5. What would make you distrust or reject a purchased template?
6. Would a working Next.js/Supabase starter at $39–$49 save enough time to buy? Why or why not?

Record: date, persona (freelancer / small agency / template buyer), current tools, must-haves, objections, exact phrases, intent (`none` / `curious` / `test` / `buy`).

Count as intent only: paid preorder, request to test, detailed buying objection, or introduction to a buyer.

## Scorecard (end of Week 2)

| Signal                    | Minimum     |
| ------------------------- | ----------- |
| Relevant conversations    | 30          |
| Strong purchase intent    | 5           |
| Qualified waitlist emails | 10          |

Fewer than 3 strong responses: do not complete the product unchanged. Narrow or change the niche; write that in `DECISIONS.md`.

## Landing page rules

Must show: outcome-led promise, mockup screenshots, proposed stack, expected price, early-access form. Lead with saved time, not a technology wall.
